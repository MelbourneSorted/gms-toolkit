The GMS Toolkit

Useful little tools for everyday life. Simple instructions, no digging around and no account required.

Sign a PDF

Add a handwritten or typed signature to a PDF and save a new copy.

Merge PDFs

Combine several PDF files into one document.

Photo to PDF

Turn JPG or PNG images into a simple PDF.

Make a QR Code

Create a QR code for a website, phone number or short piece of text.

Resize an Image

Make an image smaller by choosing its new width.

Reduce PDF Size

Create a smaller image-based copy of a PDF for easier uploading or emailing.

Free and private by design. GMS does not charge to use these Toolkit tools. Files you select are processed in your browser and the contents are not sent to or stored by Get Melbourne Sorted. Finished files are downloaded by your browser to the device or location you choose. GMS cannot control access to your device or Downloads folder, so use a private device and check where sensitive files are saved.
