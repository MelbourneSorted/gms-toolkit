Sign a PDF

Add a visible signature to a PDF, then save a new copy.

Your document stays on your device. This tool reads and edits the file in your browser. It does not intentionally send your PDF to GMS.
How it works
  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Draw your signature or type your name.
  3. Choose the page and place the signature.
  4. Download your signed copy.

Draw with your mouse, finger or trackpad.

Page 1 of 1

Drag the signature into position. Use the green dot to resize it.

Important: This adds a visible electronic signature. It does not create a certificate-based cryptographic digital signature. Check whether the organisation receiving your document has particular signing requirements.
